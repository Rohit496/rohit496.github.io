Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Salesforce Hunt Autopilot ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Salesforce Hunt YouTube channel to manage that channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the app reads and manages the owner's own channel: videos and their metadata, comments and replies, playlists, captions, and channel analytics.

How the data is used

Only to manage the owner's channel: replying to comments, writing video descriptions, chapters and tags, uploading videos the owner approved, and preparing performance reports for the owner.

Sharing and storage

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Reports are kept in the owner's private workspace. Access tokens are stored privately and never published.

Public comment data

Public comments left on the channel are read only to reply to them on the owner's behalf.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
